Onboarding
Placeholder art (emoji) and silent audio are stand-ins labelled illustrative in the repo — production illustrations and recorded voice arrive via the CMS workflow (docs/12). Spoken labels here use your browser’s speech synthesis as a stand-in for the recorded child-friendly voice.
