# 8. Privacy Notices (drafts — require Australian legal review before publication)

## 8.1 Parent privacy notice (plain English)

**Little Hands — Privacy Notice (draft vX, not yet legally reviewed)**

**The short version:** your child's play stays on your device. We don't ask for your child's name (a nickname is fine), we don't collect emails for children, we show no ads, and there's no chat. You can delete everything, any time, in Parent Settings.

**What the app stores, and where**
- On your device only: the nickname and age range you choose, preferences you set, your child's artwork, stars and stickers, and simple play information (which activities were played, for how long, and how accurate tracing was). This never leaves the device unless *you* export it.
- Through Apple or Google (not us): your subscription, handled by your app store account. We never see your card details.
- If you email support: your email address and message, kept up to 2 years.

**What we never collect:** your child's full name or date of birth, photos, voice, location, contacts, biometrics, advertising identifiers, or anything typed by your child. No advertising of any kind. No selling of information. No AI training on your child's information or artwork.

**Device backups:** if you use iCloud/Google backup, the app's data is included in *your* encrypted device backup under your control.

**Your controls (Parent Settings → Privacy & Data):** view a summary of stored data; export it; correct it; delete all child data instantly. Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the device.

**Access, correction, complaints:** contact support@… (placeholder). If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). Nothing in this notice limits your rights under the Privacy Act 1988 or Australian Consumer Law.

**Changes:** we'll show you an in-app summary of any material change before it takes effect. Notice version and date shown here.

## 8.2 Child-friendly privacy explanation

Shown as an illustrated, narrated card (audio + pictures; text for early readers), reachable from the parent area and the child help character:

> **Your pictures stay here.** 🖼️
> Everything you make lives on this tablet — like a toy box at home.
> **We don't know your name.** You have a fun nickname, and that's all.
> **No strangers.** Nobody can talk to you in this app, and you can't talk to anyone.
> **Grown-ups are in charge.** Your grown-up can see your stars and can tidy everything away if you ask.

(Design note: 5 short spoken lines, one per screen tap, friendly character narration; complies with the draft Children's Code expectation of age-appropriate transparency.)

## 8.3 Notice display rules

- Collection summary shown during onboarding **before** profile creation (APP 5); full notice always available in Parent Settings and on the store listing pages.
- Notice version + acknowledgement timestamp stored locally; material changes re-prompt the parent.
- Store privacy labels (Apple nutrition label, Google Data safety) must be regenerated from docs/07 §7.1 whenever the inventory changes — release checklist item (docs/14).
