# 6. Australian Compliance Register

**Date checked: 15 July 2026.** Instrument status was verified against the official and secondary sources listed per row and in §6.3. This register is an engineering/product compliance analysis, **not legal advice**. Every conclusion below is marked for confirmation by qualified Australian legal counsel before release. Nothing in this repository claims the app *is* legally compliant.

**Status key:** LEG = legislation in force · CODE-DRAFT = enforceable code in development (exposure draft) · GUID = regulator guidance (not directly enforceable) · STORE = app-store policy (contractual) · REFORM = passed but not yet commenced.

## 6.2 Cross-cutting engineering controls already in this design

Minimal collection (nickname + age band only); local-first storage; no ads of any kind; no third-party SDKs touching data; parental gate; no child free-text; encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (OS sandbox + optional SQLCipher); deletion feature with tests; no location/contacts/biometric collection; notifications parent-only and local; copy linting against pressure/urgency language.
